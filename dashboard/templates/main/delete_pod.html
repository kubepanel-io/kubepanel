What happens when you delete a pod?

  • The pod will be terminated immediately
  • If the pod is managed by a Deployment, StatefulSet, or ReplicaSet, Kubernetes will automatically create a new pod to replace it
  • Any unsaved data in the pod's containers will be lost

Safe to delete: Pods in Failed, Succeeded, or Completed state are safe to delete as they are no longer running.
